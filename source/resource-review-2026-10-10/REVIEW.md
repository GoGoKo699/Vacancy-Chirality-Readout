# Resource assessment and allocation

## What changed

The candidate no longer needs full number-resolving detection to recognize a return at finite repulsion. With at most three atoms, the total site-parity outcome (0,1,1,1) is exactly the accepted occupancy projector. This supports the four calibrated settings already established for arbitrary-input chirality estimation. It does not change the Hamiltonian or rely on rare doublons.

That observation separates the attainable tasks. A final destructive image can estimate input chirality. Retaining the claimed entanglement additionally requires a spin-coherent accepted measurement. Identical return effects can have a fully dephasing, fully separable retained output. Even in the hard-core limit, repeated phase-damping readout removes the infinite-round survival plateau; the finite-U survival limitation is not the only issue. Equal-rate loss with no gains is rejected by the same parity event and reduces success by an exactly calculable scalar, under that particular loss model.

## Strongest matched comparison

The closest circuit paper already provides a six-local-Pauli measurement baseline requiring no entangling measurement gate. Avoiding a controlled-SWAP therefore does not alone prove simpler hardware. The vacancy protocol removes spin-addressed measurement controls in its declared runtime primitives, but requires coherent flux hopping, a localized vacancy acting as a motional ancilla, known parameters, and parity readout. Calibration and trial survival are costs.

Under the common chi normalization, the existing finite-U16 four-setting example has n Var=.8759835 on the maximally mixed input with the declared shot allocation; direct Pauli products give .5625 and the controlled-cycle Hadamard test gives .25 on that input. These are matched ideal sampling statements, not hardware cycle-time measurements or optimized comparisons over all vacancy protocols. The strict-hard-core implementation has a smaller worst-case bound than separately sampled Pauli strings, but its physical resources are not thereby priced equally.

Primary experiments establish parity imaging, synthetic flux, atom survival, and, in a different dual-isotope architecture, spin-coherence preservation during other-atom imaging. They do not establish the simultaneous apparatus needed for the retained-state version of this proposal. In particular spin-insensitive outcome labels and atom retention do not certify spin-coherent back-action.

## Decision

GO for consolidation of the bounded **native parity-based chirality readout** result. Keep the accepted-state operation and its finite-U reference-uniform bound, but distinguish it from input-expectation estimation and from a demonstrated entangler. The useful result is an explicit implementation with stated resources and exact finite-U reconstruction, not a new chirality principle or a universally better detector.

The next action is a claims-first account of this finite model and its matched resources, with no automatic extension of the apparatus. No repository has been selected, created, or accessed in this review. Existing repository permissions are not transferred. This decision is not a strong editorial forecast, a hardware advantage, or a priority certificate.
