## Search and inference boundaries

Searches covered vacancy/hole-return chirality, controlled-cycle measurements, parity/number imaging, internal-spin preservation and atom survival, flux plaquettes, and multi-particle internal-state phases in spatial counts. Unrelated chiral-induced-spin-selectivity and nuclear spectroscopy results were not treated as protocol matches. No directly covering native vacancy-return and finite-U calibration protocol was identified in the inspected material. Coverage is targeted, not exhaustive priority clearance.

The assertion that the reviewed papers do not jointly validate the full apparatus is about this source set; it is not a global experimental no-go. Conversely, a good imaging fidelity, fast image, small spin-flip probability, or atom survival probability is insufficient to infer an unmeasured coherence-preserving instrument. That logical distinction is proved explicitly in the new note through two instruments with the same outcome effects.
